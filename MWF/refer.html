MWF Full Models UML Diagram

Staff

  • user (OneToOne → Django User)
  • role (manager / attendant)
  • phone
  • email

Customer

  • name
  • contact
  • email
  • address

Supplier

  • name
  • contact
  • email
  • address

Product

  • name
  • category (Wood / Furniture)
  • unit_price
  • supplier (FK → Supplier)

Stock

  • product (FK → Product)
  • quantity
  • warehouse
  • last_updated

Sale

  • customer (FK → Customer)
  • staff (FK → Staff)
  • date
  • total_amount
  • transport_fee (5%)

SaleItem

  • sale (FK → Sale)
  • stock (FK → Stock)
  • quantity
  • subtotal

Relationships:

  • Supplier → Product (One-to-Many)
  • Product → Stock (One-to-One or One-to-Many for multiple locations)
  • Stock → SaleItem (One-to-Many)
  • Sale → SaleItem (One-to-Many)
  • Customer → Sale (One-to-Many)
  • Staff → Sale (One-to-Many)
